Delete Your Data

Last updated: 3 October 2026
Applies to Color Block: HuePop (com.canalgrid.colorbox), published by Canal Grid

HuePop has no accounts

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. We never create an account for you, and we hold no profile, no server-side save, and no personal information about you anywhere.

Everything the game remembers is stored on your own phone, which means you can delete all of it yourself, immediately, without asking us.

Option 1 — Reset progress inside the game

  1. Open HuePop and tap Settings on the home screen
  2. Tap Reset Progress, then confirm

This erases your best score, every Daily Puzzle result and any unfinished game. Your sound, haptics, voice and motion settings are kept. It cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Delete everything (uninstall)

Uninstalling the app erases every piece of data it created on your phone, settings included.

  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Go to Apps and select Color Block: HuePop
  3. Tap Uninstall, then confirm

You can also press and hold the HuePop icon on your home screen and choose Uninstall. There is no backup on our side to restore it from.

If Android backup to your Google Account is turned on, a copy of the game's data may also be in that backup and could be restored if you reinstall. You can remove it in your device's backup settings or at drive.google.com/drive/backups — it belongs to your Google Account, and we cannot see or delete it.

Option 3 — Clear the app's storage but keep it installed

  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Go to Apps and select Color Block: HuePop
  3. Tap Storage
  4. Tap Clear data (shown as Clear storage on some devices), then confirm

The app stays installed, and everything — including settings — resets to the very beginning.

Option 4 — Ask us to do it

You do not need our help to delete anything, but you are welcome to ask us to confirm it in writing. This is also the route to take if you believe we hold data about you.

  1. Email vmwnpela@gmail.com with the subject Account and data deletion request
  2. Tell us the name of the app (HuePop) and, if you like, the device you play on
  3. We reply within 30 days. In almost every case the reply confirms that we hold no data about you, and that the steps above are all that is required.

Retention period: we retain none of this data, for any length of time, because none of it ever reaches us. There is no server, no database and no backup of ours holding your information, so there is no retention window to wait out.

Played the earlier Color Box game?

Versions before 2.0.0 (Color Box: Tidy the Shelves) showed ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), which processed your device's advertising identifier. That identifier belongs to your device and was never received by us. You can reset or remove it yourself under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device, and ask the ad provider directly via the Unity / ironSource privacy policy. HuePop itself uses no advertising ID.
