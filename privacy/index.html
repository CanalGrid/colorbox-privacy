HuePop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026
Applies to the Android app Color Block: HuePop (com.canalgrid.colorbox), version 2.0.0 and later

The short version

HuePop does not ask you to sign in, shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, and uses no analytics or tracking. It collects nothing about you. Your scores and settings are saved only on your own device, and the game sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

1. Who we are

Color Block: HuePop is developed and published by Canal Grid ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with data, and what it deliberately does not do.

HuePop replaces our earlier game Color Box: Tidy the Shelves under the same Google Play listing. If you still have a version of Color Box older than 2.0.0 installed, see Earlier versions below.

2. What is stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, using Android's standard app storage. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we cannot read it.

  • Your best score
  • For each Daily Puzzle you play: whether you completed it, your best score, how many attempts you made, and when you first completed it
  • An unfinished game, so you can pick up where you left off
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, voice, reduce motion

Device backup: if you have turned on Android's backup to your Google Account, Android may include this game data in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google Account and is managed by Google under your settings. We have no access to it.

Deleting it is simple: use Settings → Reset Progress in the game, or uninstall the app. There is no copy on any server of ours, so there is nothing else to request or erase. Step-by-step instructions, and how to ask us for written confirmation, are on the Delete your data page.

3. What we do not collect

To be explicit about it, HuePop has no:

  • account, login, or registration of any kind
  • collection of your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • access to your photos, camera, microphone, files, or location
  • advertising, and no use of your device's advertising ID
  • in-app purchases or payment details
  • analytics or crash-reporting service tracking how you play
  • notifications sent to you
  • sale or sharing of personal information with anyone

4. The weekly scoreboard

The scoreboard is worked out entirely on your device from your own Daily Puzzle scores. Your score is never uploaded, and no one else can see it. The other names on the board are computer-generated players, not real people, so no information about anyone is exchanged.

5. Voice

When Voice is on, the game speaks short words of praise such as "Nice!" or "Combo Blast!" using your device's built-in text-to-speech service. The only thing passed to that service is the fixed word being spoken — never anything about you. The text-to-speech service is part of your device (for example, Speech Services by Google) and runs under its provider's own terms. You can turn Voice off at any time in the game's Settings.

6. Internet access

The game is fully playable offline, and it does not send any data over the internet. The only time it goes online is when you tap Privacy Policy in Settings, which opens this page. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any website host, may record standard request information such as your IP address under GitHub's privacy statement.

7. Children

HuePop is a puzzle game suitable for a general audience. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and shows no ads. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will look into it.

9. Earlier versions (Color Box: Tidy the Shelves)

Versions before 2.0.0 were a different game, Color Box: Tidy the Shelves. Those versions showed ads delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it mediates, which could process your device's advertising identifier, coarse device and network information, and your interactions with the ads. They could also schedule reminder notifications on your device. Updating to HuePop removes all of this: version 2.0.0 contains no advertising code and sends no notifications.

Any ad data from those versions was held by the ad providers, not by us, under their own policies: Unity / ironSource privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently — for example by adding ads, purchases or an online scoreboard — we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above before that version is released. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
