Color Box

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026
Applies to the Android app Color Box: Tidy the Shelves (com.canalgrid.colorbox)

The short version

Color Box does not ask you to sign in and does not collect your name, email address, or any other information that identifies you. Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device. The one exception is advertising: the ads shown in the free version are served by a third party that uses your device's advertising ID.

1. Who we are

Color Box is developed and published by Canal Grid ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with data, and what it deliberately does not do.

2. What is stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, using Android's standard app storage. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we cannot read it.

  • Which level you are on, and the furthest level you have unlocked
  • Your star rating for each level, and your best Speed Run time for each world
  • Whether you have completed the tutorial
  • Your settings: language, background music, sound effects, vibration, reduced motion, colour symbols
  • Whether ads have been switched off

Deleting all of it is simple: uninstalling the app removes this data permanently. There is no copy on any server, so there is nothing else to request or erase.

3. What we do not collect

To be explicit about it, Color Box has no:

  • account, login, or registration of any kind
  • collection of your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • access to your photos, camera, microphone, files, or location
  • analytics or crash-reporting service tracking how you play
  • sale or sharing of personal information with anyone

4. Advertising

The free version of Color Box shows a small banner and, if you choose to tap Solve, an optional video ad that you can watch to reveal a level's solution. Watching an ad is always your choice — the game is fully playable without it.

Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it mediates. To select and measure ads, these providers may process your device's advertising identifier, coarse device and network information, and your interactions with the ads themselves. This processing is carried out by them, not by us, and is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Unity / ironSource privacy policy
  • How Google uses information from partner apps

Controlling ad personalisation

On Android you can limit or delete your advertising ID at any time under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact wording varies by device). Removing ads inside the app also stops ads from being requested.

5. Reminder notifications

Color Box can send you an occasional reminder to come back and play. These reminders are scheduled entirely on your device — no message is sent from a server, and no information about you leaves your phone. On Android 13 and later the app asks for notification permission the first time it runs; declining it costs you nothing else in the game, and you can turn reminders off later in your Android notification settings.

6. Children

Color Box is a puzzle game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If ads are shown, they are subject to the ad providers' own policies on age-appropriate advertising. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will look into it.
