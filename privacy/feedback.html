Before you write

A few things answer most messages on the spot:

  • "No more moves" appeared? That is the end of a game, not a bug — it happens when none of the pieces you are holding fits anywhere on the board. Leave room for big pieces and clear lines early.
  • Need to stop mid-game? Tap pause and choose Save & Exit; Continue on the home screen picks up where you left off.
  • Wondering about the other players on the scoreboard? HuePop works fully offline, so the other names are computer-generated players, not real people. Your ranking is worked out on your phone and is never uploaded.
