Before you write

Two things solve most reports on the spot:

  • Stuck with no moves left? That is part of the puzzle, not a bug — every board can be solved from the start, but a wrong move can still paint you into a corner. Tap Undo to step back, or Restart to reset the level.
  • Cannot find the next move? The lightbulb button gives a hint, and Solve plays out the full solution for you.
